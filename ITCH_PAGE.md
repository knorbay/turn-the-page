# TURN THE PAGE

**The page is alive. The hand holding the pencil is not always on your side.**

TURN THE PAGE is a hand-drawn, combat-heavy 2D action game set inside a school
notebook. Fight from page to page as a stick figure while the Artist outside
the paper redraws weapons, terrain, enemies, and sometimes you.

Each page changes the rules. Draw an Ink Katana as a notebook ronin. Carry a
six-shooter and double barrel through a ledger-paper West. Bank energy shots
around orbital diagrams. Breach a carbon-copy office with an agent's suppressed
pistol. Then face the version of the final boss that your own play style created.

The beta contains five pages, 23 combat rooms, 20 ordinary enemy types, six
named bosses, twelve Lost Sketch techniques, four finale variants, and one very
large baby face that makes the Artist reconsider fairness.

## Controls

- Move: A/D or arrow keys
- Jump: Space
- Attack: F, J, or left mouse
- Dash / perfect return: Shift, K, or right mouse
- Reload: R
- Change acquired weapon: Q, mouse wheel, controller shoulder/D-pad
- Examine a Lost Sketch: E
- Pause: Esc

Controller support is included. The game saves automatically.

## Beta note

This is the first public beta. Please report the page/room name, what you were

# 0.42 — Polished pages, dangerous margins

The notebook has grown since the 0.20 release. This update brings the recent combat, boss music and ending work together with a fresh polish pass.

Menus now highlight one choice at a time and show the correct controller prompts. The Artist chooses better companion tools for ranged weapons such as the folding crossbow and orbit saw. Automatic gifts give way when you interact or enter a new fight, then return during a quieter stretch; your held weapon and ammunition stay yours.

Boss encounters begin with the Artist drawing the actual figure while the camera moves closer. The fight waits until the drawing is complete, and route signs stay out of the close-up. Each of the twelve boss cues uses a different licensed recording, with shorter, less oppressive passages chosen for its setting.

Four more optional guardians join the Cloud Kite: Brass Tumbleweed, Orbit Crab, Carbon Hound and Draft Moth. Their two-phase attacks leave clear openings, and their rewards bring the collection to 19 permanent sketches and runes. Normal fights use more varied groups; the folding crossbow and orbit saw add different ways to answer them.

There is also a proper last page to play. Draw three memories and sign the notebook in the Afterword. Once the campaign is complete, Replay Pages takes you back to any chapter or the closing page while keeping your learned sketches.

Menus, combat messages and notebook text support English, Turkish, German and Italian. Repeated jump signs and extra explanation boxes have been reduced so the page has more room to breathe.

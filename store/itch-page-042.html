The page is alive. The hand holding the pencil is not always on your side.

Turn the Page is a single-player action platformer inside a school notebook. You are a small ink figure; the Artist watches your play, draws weapons and revises the route. Five pages take you through ronin gardens, Western rails, space, a carbon-copy city and discarded drafts.

Read the red warnings, find an opening and time your dash to return incoming shots.

Inside the notebook

  • Five chapters with named bosses, route puzzles and changing arsenals.
  • Five optional mini bosses: Cloud Kite, Brass Tumbleweed, Orbit Crab, Carbon Hound and Draft Moth.
  • 19 permanent sketch and rune rewards, a back-page collection and achievements.
  • Different weapon roles: blades, firearms, a folding crossbow, an orbit saw and an oversized pencil.
  • Drawn boss entrances and 12 distinct licensed boss music recordings.
  • A playable afterword: draw three memories, sign the notebook and revisit completed pages with your learned sketches.

English, Türkçe, Deutsch and Italiano are available from the title screen and settings. Checkpoints save automatically. Separate training preserves campaign progress; controller support is included.

0.42 — Notebook polish

Clearer menus and controller prompts, cleaner boss framing and better companion tools from the Artist. Automatic gifts wait when you interact or enter a fight; your held weapon and ammunition stay yours.

Public beta, free / pay what you want. Existing campaign saves are supported. Bug reports are welcome: please include the version, page and room.

Controls

  • Move: A/D or Left/Right   Jump: Space/W/Up
  • Attack: F/J or left click   Dash / return: Shift/K or right click
  • Interact: E   Reload: R   Weapon: Q or mouse wheel
  • Drop through: S/Down + jump   Pause: Esc   Fullscreen: F11

Release jump early for a shorter jump. Mouse attacks aim at the pointer.

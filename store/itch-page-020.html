The page is alive. The hand holding the pencil is not always on your side.

Turn the Page is an action game inside a school notebook. You are a small ink figure; the Artist outside the paper can redraw your weapons, revise the route, interrupt a fight, or erase your failed drawing and try again.

0.20 — Living Pages

The latest macOS build brings the notebook's revisions into the route itself. Trace a bridge and erase its margin. Climb a torn paper shelf and dash through a Wanted poster's high seam. Carry a fading star over satellite ledges before its signal goes out. The Artist responds to your solutions and to how you play.

  • Five distinct pages, 25 required encounters, 27 ordinary enemy types and six named bosses.
  • 28 location-specific notebook drawings: blade studies, a Western station, space wreckage, carbon-copy files and the last classroom.
  • Three physical route puzzles, twelve optional Lost Sketch challenges and 28 achievements.
  • Distinct weapon silhouettes, handling, attack strokes, recoil and reload poses. Katana, Bowie, six-shooter, double barrel, orbital tools, carbon rifle and an oversized drawn pencil all have their own role.
  • Multi-phase boss rules, the Lost Expedition reversal and four finale variants shaped by your fighting style.
  • Pencil, paper and eraser feedback, a layered desk soundtrack, page curls, and drawings that visibly break and fade when defeated.

Current release: 0.20.0 for Windows, Linux and macOS. Existing campaign saves remain compatible.

Controls

  • Move: A/D or arrow keys
  • Jump: Space; hold for a higher jump
  • Attack: F, J or left mouse
  • Dash / perfect return: Shift, K or right mouse
  • Drop through a thin platform: S + Space
  • Reload: R
  • Change acquired weapon: Q or mouse wheel
  • Interact, examine a sketch or reply to the Artist: E
  • Pause: Esc; fullscreen: F11

Controller support and automatic saves are included. This is a public beta. If something breaks, please include the page and room name in your report.

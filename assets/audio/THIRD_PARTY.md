# Audio source ledger

**0.40 runtime:** twelve individually credited cinematic boss recordings, the first releases' six CC0 exploration/combat recordings, four original procedural exploration/combat WAVs, the 0.34 notebook sound bank, and established pencil/erase/blade recordings. The five original procedural boss WAVs remain as missing-asset fallbacks. Earlier music experiments and their source/licence history remain below and in their earlier releases.

Sources and licences are listed per entry. Current external boss music is **CC BY 4.0**; the six original exploration/combat recordings use **CC0 1.0**. Established non-music recordings use CC0; archived firearm recordings retain their CC-BY 3.0 attribution and licence.

## Selected 0.40 boss music

Each of the twelve boss identities has its own recorded loop. Full recording titles, composers, official source links, licence links and audio modifications are in [boss/CREDITS.md](boss/CREDITS.md), and are also available in the game's music credits page. [boss/manifest.json](boss/manifest.json) records source hashes, excerpt boundaries, processing, runtime durations and asset hashes.

The selected recordings are by **PeriTune / Sei Mutsuki**, **Scott Buckley**, and **Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)**, under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The PeriTune works selected here were published before March 2026 and retain CC BY 4.0, as explained in the composer's [licence terms](https://peritune.com/about/). Later PeriTune works use different terms and are not included in this release.

Recordings use either a composer-provided loop or an edited excerpt with a 250 ms loop crossfade. All receive level adjustment and a 22050 Hz stereo OGG transcode, without changing pitch or tempo. Individual modifications are identified in the credits and manifest. Playback uses these mastered recordings directly, without adding synthesized percussion. The historical five page boss loops below are no longer the selected boss playlist.

## Original procedural combat bank

All root-level `sfx_*.wav` files, including the `_v2`/`_v3`/`_v4` alternate
takes and the weapon, enemy-material, and boss-phase cues added in the combat
polish pass, are original deterministic renders from this repository's
`audio_composer.py`. They contain no downloaded samples and introduce no new
third-party license obligation. The same is true of `classroom_babble.wav`
and the locally composed `page_*_*.wav` score fallbacks.
